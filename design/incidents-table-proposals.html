E — Grouped by severity

The tier badge is the single most decision-relevant signal on this list, so it becomes the grouping key, not just a per-row label. Rows stay chronological within each group.

Recommended

B — it fixes every concrete problem in A (alignment, wrapping, missing headers) without changing the reading model the manager already has. E is the more powerful idea for a genuinely busy night, worth keeping in reserve if incident volume regularly climbs past ~15 and severity needs to be findable at a glance rather than read row by row.
